     * GET /open-games/me/results — cross-venue: the caller's recent open
     * games (30-day lookback) with their result state. Entries:
     * `{ gameId, venueId, venueGroupClassesId, name, startTime,
     * participants: [{ uvpId, playerId, displayName, initials, avatarUrl,
     * publicProfileSlug, isMe }], result: OpenGameResult|null, reportable }`.
     * The ONLY surface where roster participants carry ids — don't leak them
     * elsewhere.
     *
     * `publicProfileSlug` (PLA-3946) is nullable and NOT a guarantee of the
     * deployment: a league older than that ticket omits it entirely, which the
     * post-game buddy suggestion reads as "nobody here can be followed" and
     * drops in silence. Treat a missing field as null, never as an error.
     * POST /open-games/{id}/result — report a loose result: explicit winner
     * side + optional sets (max 7, ints 0–99, no sport-format validation).
     * Payload: `{ homeUvpIds: [1..2], awayUvpIds: [1..2], winnerSide:
     * 'home'|'away', sets?: [{ home, away }] }`. Sides are validated
     * server-side against the LIVE roster (disjoint (1,1) or (2,2),
     * reporter on a side). Re-report by the original submitter overwrites
     * and restarts the auto-confirm clock.
     *
     * Error codes: OPEN_GAME_RESULTS_DISABLED (403),
     * OPEN_GAME_RESULT_INVALID_SIDES / _NOT_ENOUGH_PLAYERS (400),
     * OPEN_GAME_RESULT_NOT_CONFIRMED_GAME / _NOT_STARTED /
     * _DEADLINE_EXPIRED / _ALREADY_EXISTS (409),
     * OPEN_GAME_RESULT_TRAINER_GAME (403),
     * OPEN_GAME_ROSTER_UNAVAILABLE (502).
