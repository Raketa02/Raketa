## Push permission asks (code)
- com.playtomic.modules.home.presenter.HomeTabPresenter, on Home tab viewLoad: unless key-value 'onboarding_finish_notification_permission' is TRUE, and when 'notification_permission_prompt_count' >= 1 (NotifPermissionPromptDisplayControl default threshold 1) and status != authorized, calls INotificationManager.h(push_popup_title, push_popup_description, ...). Otherwise increments 'notification_permission_prompt_count' (once per presenter) and returns.
- com.playtomic.general.manager.notification.PermissionBridgeNotificationManager.h(): Android < 13 -> authorized (no ask). notDetermined -> pre-prompt then system request. denied -> DialogIntent(title=push_denied 'Turn on Notifications', message=push_denied_action, buttons yes / not_now); yes opens the system settings. Gated by a static boolean f (initially true).
- Settings > Notifications screens (NotificationSettingsViewState / ProfileMenuSettingsNotificationsViewState) carry showNotificationPermissionBanner and a PromptNotificationsPermissions action.
