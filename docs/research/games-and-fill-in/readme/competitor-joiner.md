## Sections (`###` headings)

**1. Who may join**
- An account, a level and a public profile are needed to join
- Phone verification gates chat and adding players, not joining alone
- A blocked player cannot join
- The match's gender is checked at join
- Competitive matches admit only players in range
- Requesting a spot needs every player's yes and holds no seat

**2. Finding a game**
- Find a match by sport, place and time
- What a match card shows
- No spot count and no deadline on the card
- The match page shows whether the court is booked
- Priority alerts cover last-minute cancellations by place, date and time
- Pushes about people you follow, and Golden Matches
- Community Matches sit beside open matches and take no payment
- Chat groups are private lists of up to 200 players

**3. Joining and paying**
- She pays her own spot online at join, charged at once
- The price per spot can differ between joiners
- The Polish service fee on an open-match spot
- Premium removes the fee
- The checkout shows the cancellation block, never a passed deadline
- Adding a card at checkout takes four screens
- Ways to pay in Poland: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Club Wallet; no BLIK
- 3-D Secure and failed payments
- Joining with a partner
- Joining a private booking turned public
- A joiner the club adds pays in the app or owes a debt
- On joining: a confirmation email and the club's message

**4. While the game fills**
- The court is booked in stages
- A match still short about an hour before start is cancelled by Anemone
- Players are not told when someone joins or leaves an open match
- The match chat is public

**5. Leaving**
- A match not yet full can be left any time before it starts
- Leaving refunds in full, the service fee included
- Inside 24 hours of a full match only the club can let her go
- A seat can't be handed on; a swap is the club's job
- No waiting list; a freed seat simply reopens
- Rejoining after leaving is not described

**6. Refunds**
- Refunds go back to the card in days, to a wallet at once
- Weather and a closed club are the club's call
- A club may cancel without notice, and may refund to its wallet

**7. On the day and after**
- The court PIN is in the booking and its email
- Results within 7 days, confirmed after 24 hours
- A no-show is reported to support and charged as a debt
- The Terms' no-show covers late arrival, early leaving and a replaced player
- No attendance score
- A chargeback becomes a debt
- The receipt is the full share or only the fee
- Support is a chat, in nine languages, not Polish
- No Kraków club on Playtomic

**8. What joiners complain about**
- A late leaver blocks the seat in a full match
- Players ask for a queue for freed seats
- Not told about last-minute cancellations
- No-shows go unpunished, or hit the wrong person
- Refunds that never arrive
- Matches that don't fill, and levels that don't fit
- The fee on a spot that is the only way in
- Finding your own next game
- Support is a bot
