# Playtomic, seen by the joiner

The main competitor, Playtomic, from the side of the player who joins a game someone else started:
finding it, joining, paying, leaving, refunds, late drop-outs and no-shows. It is the competitor the
spec compares itself with (the-mechanics.md Appendix B). It has no club in Kraków
([No Kraków club](#no-kraków-club-on-playtomic)).

**Sources.** Help articles were read in full through the help centres' public Zendesk API (the
pages answer 403); links point at the human page. Terms from playtomic.com; reviews from the App
Store's review feeds (PL, GB, IE, ES); Reddit refuses automated reads. No screenshot, so no
`[MEASURED]`.

§5, §7.2–§7.9, read 2026-10-06/07); facts carried from it keep their dates. Every article a grade

## 1. Who may join

### An account, a level and a public profile are needed to join
- **[POLICY]** "in order to join a public match, you must have a Playtomic account"; "No one can be added to a match without a Playtomic account." [Playtomic help: How to sign up for an Open Match](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19832151055121-How-to-sign-up-for-an-Open-Match-Padel-Tennis) (updated 2026-10-03) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** A level, from a one-time questionnaire per sport, is needed before joining open matches. [Playtomic help: How to create your account](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831938095633-How-to-create-your-account-in-Playtomic) — read 2026-10-07
- **[POLICY]** "You won't be able to participate in public activities like open matches or tournaments with a private profile." [Playtomic help: Playtomic Community](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831992618769-Playtomic-Community) — read 2026-10-07
- **[POLICY]** Install to a first open match is about 22 screens (3 account, ~9 levelling, ~8 to find and join, 2 to add a card), counted from Playtomic's help images. `games-and-fill-in-market.md` §7.9 S03-G1 — read 2026-10-07

### Phone verification gates chat and adding players, not joining alone
- **[POLICY]** SMS verification is needed for "starting a chat, following another user, creating a group, inviting or adding players to a match, uploading results, or setting up a community match". [Playtomic help: How to verify your phone number](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/51110112972561-How-to-verify-your-phone-number) — read 2026-10-07

### A blocked player cannot join
- **[POLICY]** "If someone in an Open Match has blocked a player, that blocked player will not be able to join the match." Blocking someone already in it: **[ABSENT]**. [Playtomic help: How to block and/or report a Player](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831797811729-How-to-block-and-or-report-a-Player) — read 2026-10-07

### The match's gender is checked at join
- **[POLICY]** The first player sets All Players, Only Women, Only Men or Mixed; it can't be changed, and a player of the wrong gender "will be unable to participate". [Playtomic help: Gender of the match in Open Matches](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831467158801-Gender-of-the-match-in-Open-Matches) — read 2026-10-07

### Competitive matches admit only players in range
- **[POLICY]** Competitive: "Only players who are within the level requested inside the match can participate, but players who are out of range can request the spot". Casual: "All players can join (the level range doesn't matter!)". The type is fixed at creation. [Playtomic help: Casual vs Competitive Public Matches](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831974052625-Casual-vs-Competitive-Public-Matches) (updated 2026-06-09) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** A player-made competitive range is −0.25 / +0.75 around the first player. `games-and-fill-in-market.md` S04-G5 — read 2026-10-07

### Requesting a spot needs every player's yes and holds no seat
- **[POLICY]** Every enrolled player is notified; "it is enough for one player to reject"; once all accept, a *Book spot* button appears and she must pay. "Spots are not reserved during the approval process, so another player could take yours before you finish booking." [Playtomic help: How to request a spot in an open match](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19832027031569-How-to-request-a-spot-in-a-open-match) (updated 2026-05-28) — read 2026-10-08

## 2. Finding a game

### Find a match by sport, place and time
- **[POLICY]** Home → *Find a match* → sport → where → when → results; "You join the match alone and are matched with opponents of similar levels." Padel and tennis only. [Playtomic help: How to sign up for an Open Match](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19832151055121-How-to-sign-up-for-an-Open-Match-Padel-Tennis) — read 2026-10-08

### What a match card shows
- **[POLICY]** Date and hour; *Competitive* or *Friendly* with the level range; four seats two and two, each with photo or initials, first name and a one-decimal level; empty seats "+ Available"; club, distance; price per spot and length ("€12.54 / 60min"). Lists are headed "For your level", "Request your place", "Be the first player!". `games-and-fill-in-market.md` §7.2 S05-G-1, S05-G-14 — read 2026-10-07

### No spot count and no deadline on the card
- **[ABSENT]** No "2/4", "spots left" or fill-by time on any documented card; the player text names no cancellation hour. `games-and-fill-in-market.md` §7.2 S05-G-12, §7.8 S05-G-2 — read 2026-10-07

### The match page shows whether the court is booked
- **[POLICY]** Columns *Gender*, *Level*, *Price*; "Open Match" with "✓ Court booked" once a court is held; seats with A and B; *Calendar*, *Chat*, *Join match*. Court type shows only at checkout. `games-and-fill-in-market.md` §7.2 S05-G-1, S05-G-15 — read 2026-10-07

### Community Matches sit beside open matches and take no payment
- **[POLICY]** A Community Match is a game at a court outside Playtomic: no booking or payment in the app; the creator books, pays and coordinates "splitting the court costs"; "Players who join your Community Match will not be charged any fee in the app." Open matches show the Playtomic icon and a price per person; community matches "display no joining fee". Joiners use "Leave match"; the creator "can cancel the match … at any time". [Playtomic help: Community Match](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831629310481-Community-Match-Play-on-Any-Court-Anywhere) (updated 2026-06-19) — read 2026-10-08

### Chat groups are private lists of up to 200 players
- **[POLICY]** A player can create a chat group and "share the link of the group to promote your matches"; groups are private ("'public' chat groups coming soon"), at most 200 users. Nothing ties a group to its own matches or deals games to its members **[ABSENT]** (searched "group" in the player help). ## 4. While the game fills

### The court is booked in stages
- **[POLICY]** "1 player: The court is never reserved. 2 players: … more than 12 hours until match time. 3 players: … more than 4 hours … 4 players: Court always reserved." Poland is not among the 20 countries where one player holds it until 48 h before. [Playtomic help: Cancellation Policy for Open Matches](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831672824465-Cancellation-Policy-for-Open-Matches-Padel-Tennis) (updated 2026-09-30) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** Below the thresholds the match "isn't canceled right away. As long as the club still has an available court for that time slot, the match stays open". [Playtomic club help: How to cancel an Open Match](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535128515601-How-to-cancel-an-Open-Match) (updated 2026-10-08) — read 2026-10-08

### A match still short about an hour before start is cancelled by Anemone
- **[POLICY]** Cancelled when "No court is left available for that time slot" (another match fills first, or "private reservations always take priority over open matches that aren't full") or when it "still hasn't reached 4 players about 1 hour before the scheduled start time, even if a court happens to still be available". "'Cancelled by Anemone' means the match was cancelled automatically". On these, "or if a player leaves the match", players get "a full automatic refund (including the Playtomic service fee)". [Playtomic club help: How to cancel an Open Match](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535128515601-How-to-cancel-an-Open-Match) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** Player help names no hour ("Not enough players join before the start time"; "Our system predicts match completion based on time, club popularity, and player patterns") and says "Cancelled matches can't be restored". [Playtomic help: Cancellation Policy for Open Matches](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831672824465-Cancellation-Policy-for-Open-Matches-Padel-Tennis) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** A court the club forces onto a match means "it won't be automatically cancelled". [Playtomic club help: Understanding When Open Matches Occupy Club Calendar Slots](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535700374929) — read 2026-10-06

### Players are not told when someone joins or leaves an open match

## 5. Leaving

[Playtomic help: Cancellation Policy for Open Matches](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831672824465-Cancellation-Policy-for-Open-Matches-Padel-Tennis) — read 2026-10-08

## 6. Refunds

### Refunds go back to the card in days, to a wallet at once
- **[POLICY]** Cancelled "by you or by the Club, the money is returned to the original payment method": a card refund is processed at once but "can take 2-15 business days"; Club Wallet "instant"; a same-day cancel is a reversal. ### Reading on money at the moment of joining
- *reading:* Ivan pays Playtomic 43,99 zł the moment he joins a 40 zł spot ([fee](#the-polish-service-fee-on-an-open-match-spot)); at Rakieta his yes is free, and every card is charged 40 zł only at the last yes.

### Reading on leaving in time
- *reading:* Playtomic gives a timely leaver everything back, fee included, and lets him leave a not-full match up to the start ([leaving](#a-match-not-yet-full-can-be-left-any-time-before-it-starts)); Rakieta keeps 1,60 zł of a 40 zł share from a player who leaves before the cut-off.

### Reading on the late leaver
- *reading:* Inside 24 h of a full match Playtomic keeps both the seat and the money unless the club relents; the complaint above asks for "lose the money, reopen the seat" ([complaint](#a-late-leaver-blocks-the-seat-in-a-full-match)), which is the shape of Rakieta's Fill in.

### Reading on a game that doesn't fill
- *reading:* Playtomic takes each joiner's money and refunds it when the match is short about an hour before; Rakieta charges nobody until the game is full, and an unfilled card expires.

### Reading on no-shows
- *reading:* Playtomic's no-show is a support report within 7 days and before a result, charged as a debt; Rakieta records it in the result, with 24 h to dispute.
