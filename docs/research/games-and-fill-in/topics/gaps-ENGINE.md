# Gaps — ENGINE

Answers to the facts the ENGINE grades lacked (PRD #530), one numbered section per gap, each naming
its rule IDs. Read 2026-10-08 by the ENGINE gaps researcher. Kinds as in `../README.md`:
`[POLICY]`, `[PRICE]`, `[LAW]`, `[DOCS]`, `[MEASURED]`, `[COMPLAINT]`, `[ABSENT]` (looked for and
not found, with where). Playtomic's, Meetup's and Tinder's help centres were read through their
Zendesk article API (`/api/v2/help_center/<locale>/articles/<id>.json`), which returns the live
article and its `updated_at`; Playtomic's web pages answer 403 to a plain fetch. App Store reviews
of Playtomic: the public reviews feed (`itunes.apple.com/<cc>/rss/customerreviews/…/id=1242321076`),
20 storefronts, 2,053 reviews, read 2026-10-08. No competitor app was used first-hand: joining a
match needs an account, a phone and a payment, so nothing here is `[MEASURED]`.

## 1. A block between two people who already share a chat and a group (R-ENGINE-7)

### Playtomic: a block is "to prevent any future interactions"
- **[POLICY]** The player help: "Block - Choose this if you want to prevent any future interactions with this player"; the only effect it names is joining: "if someone in an Open Match has blocked a player, that blocked player will not be able to join the match. This also applies to: Open Matches, Private Bookings, Community Matches". The club-side copy says the same ("Block the player if they just want to stop any interaction"). [Playtomic help: How to block and/or report a Player](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831797811729-How-to-block-and-or-report-a-Player) (updated 2026-04-23), [Playtomic Manager help: How to block a user (App only)](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535339424529-How-to-block-a-user-App-only) (updated 2026-07-20) — read 2026-10-08
- **[ABSENT]** What a block does to a match the two already share, to a match chat both are in, or to whether the blocked player can still open the blocker's profile. Searched the player help for "block" and "blocked" (6 articles each; the four about blocking or chat read) and the manager help for "block a user" — read 2026-10-08
- **[ABSENT]** A search-engine summary quotes an older wording, "Blocking a player won't affect open matches … remain visible to everyone, including the blocked player"; the live article no longer says it, and the Internet Archive was offline, so it can be neither dated nor cited — read 2026-10-08

### Playtomic: chats are blocked one chat at a time
- **[POLICY]** Private chat: "you can mute or block a chat with a player … click on the three dots on the top right · Choose between mute chat or block". Match chat: "the chat is public. Any user will be able to access it"; "Remember that you can mute or block the chat if you have any problem". Blocking the chat is a separate act from blocking the player. [Playtomic help: Private chat between players](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19832055834769-Private-chat-between-players-in-Playtomic) (updated 2026-09-01), [How you can chat with players in Matches or Tournaments](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19832063135249-How-you-can-chat-with-players-in-Matches-or-Tournaments) (updated 2026-02-16) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** Playtomic's only way to hide from everyone is a private profile, and it shuts the holder out of public play: "by doing so, you will no longer be able to sign up for public Playtomic matches". [Playtomic help: How to make your profile private](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831908541329-How-to-make-your-profile-private) (updated 2026-09-29) — read 2026-10-08

### WhatsApp: a block leaves a shared group exactly as it was
- **[POLICY]** "Blocking a contact won't stop that user from seeing your messages in a group you both participate in, and you'll see their messages in that group as well. Please don't participate in groups with people you don't wish to communicate with." "You can't block a group. Alternatively, you can exit a group and block the group admin." "Blocking a contact won't remove them from your contacts list." What the block does hide: "Your last seen, online, status updates, and any changes made to your profile photo won't be visible to senders you've blocked." [WhatsApp Help Center: How to block and report someone](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1142481766359885/?locale=en_US) — read 2026-10-08

### Meetup: a block hides the profile and locks the chat; the group is untouched
- **[POLICY]** "If you block a member, they will no longer be able to contact you through messages"; the chat "becomes read-only" (web) or "shows as locked" (mobile), and "The blocked member loses access to both Active and Archived Chats"; "a person that you have blocked will not be able to see your profile details or send you any direct messages". A member is blocked from the group's Members list, and the article names no effect on either person's group membership; taking someone out of a group is the organiser's remove or ban. [Meetup Help: Block someone from contacting me](https://help.meetup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001673431-Block-someone-from-contacting-me) (updated 2026-09-09), [Meetup Help: Remove or ban a member](https://help.meetup.com/hc/en-us/articles/39256750778637-Remove-or-ban-a-member) — read 2026-10-08

### App Review 1.2 asks for "the ability to block", and says nothing about what it must hide
- **[POLICY]** Guideline 1.2: apps with user-generated content "must include … The ability to block abusive users from the service", beside a filter, a report mechanism "and timely responses to concerns", and published contact information. Nothing in 1.2 or 1.2.1 says what a block must hide (a chat, a profile, a shared group). [Apple: App Review Guidelines 1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content) — read 2026-10-08
- **[COMPLAINT]** Rejection notices developers posted quote only "A mechanism for users to block abusive users" (2021, 2024, Nov 2025); one also asks to act on reports "within 24 hours by removing the content and ejecting the user". None gives the block a scope, and no thread records Apple refusing a block because a shared chat stayed open. [Apple Developer Forums 679400](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/679400), [688227](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/688227), [749441](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/749441), [807358](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/807358); [GoodBarber: Apple rejection, user-generated content](https://goodbarber.com/help/apple-rejection-r100/apple-rejection-user-generated-content-a209) — read 2026-10-08
- **[ABSENT]** An Apple statement that a block must close a chat the two already share. Searched the guideline, Apple's forums, and the wording "Blocking should also notify the developer … remove it from the user's feed", which no indexed page carries — read 2026-10-08

## 2. Playtomic: a match on a court switched off, on a Holiday, or at a club whose plan lapses (R-ENGINE-29)

### A match is cancelled when its court "becomes unavailable"
- **[POLICY]** The player help lists, as reasons for an automatic cancellation, "Not enough players join before the start time" and "The court becomes unavailable at the club"; "Cancelled matches can't be restored". [Playtomic help: Cancellation Policy for Open Matches](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831672824465-Cancellation-Policy-for-Open-Matches-Padel-Tennis) (updated 2026-09-30) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** The club help: "When all available spots on a court are filled, the match is automatically cancelled, regardless of the number of players"; and "If a club cancels a match, it will be removed from the current schedule but will reappear in the club's list of open matches, making it available for other players to join at the same date and time." [Playtomic Manager help: Understanding When Open Matches Occupy Club Calendar Slots](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535700374929-Understanding-When-Open-Matches-Occupy-Club-Calendar-Slots) (updated 2026-10-01) — read 2026-10-08

### A Holiday and a switched-off court: only what they stop is written
- **[POLICY]** A Holiday "block[s] your schedule from online reservations", for "a specific court or the whole club"; a court has "Active Court" and "Bookable Online" switches, and "Once a court is set up and activated, it becomes visible to users on the app". [Holidays](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535276328849-How-to-add-edit-delete-Holidays) (updated 2026-03-31), [How to add/edit/delete a court](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535305323665-How-to-add-edit-delete-a-court) (updated 2026-08-25) — read 2026-10-08
- **[ABSENT]** Whether a Holiday or a court switched off ends open matches already gathering on that court or hour, and whether their players are told. Searched the manager help for "holiday", "deactivate court", "open matches cancelled"; read Holidays, Courts, Schedule basics, Schedule Settings, Shared courts and the calendar-slots article — read 2026-10-08

### A lapsed plan does not take the club offline
- **[POLICY]** "Cancelling stops the automatic renewal charge, but it does not automatically take your club offline in the Playtomic app"; "your club stays visible in the Playtomic app until our team completes the process", after which it is "no longer visible in the app and won't receive new bookings". Meanwhile Playtomic tells the club to block its calendar with a Holiday "so you don't receive any last-minute bookings". [Playtomic Manager help: How to cancel your subscription](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/50864435573009-How-to-cancel-your-subscription-in-Playtomic-Manager) (updated 2026-09-29) — read 2026-10-08
- **[POLICY]** kluby.org, used by Kraków clubs, added in 2022 "Mechanizm blokady za brak płatności za system zarządzania", a lock on a club that does not pay for the system; what it does to bookings already made is not stated. [kluby.org: Co nowego 2022](https://oferta.kluby.org/changelog?rok=2022) — read 2026-10-08
- **[ABSENT]** What happens to matches and bookings already made when a Playtomic club is set offline — read 2026-10-08

## 7. Do games offered soonest, or a few days ahead, fill more often? (R-ENGINE-16)

### Playmore steers creators three days out, on its own figure
- **[POLICY]** The creator's date picker opens later on purpose: "Pokazujemy terminy od {date} — gry zakładane 3+ dni wcześniej zbierają komplet mniej więcej 3 razy częściej. Wcześniejsze dni są pod strzałką." The "3 razy" is Playmore's own figure, with no data behind it published. Slots under 24 h carry "Mało czasu" ("Tak blisko skład zbiera się rzadko"); both hints sit behind a flag on for three clubs since 25 Sep 2026: `gaps-POST.md#playmore-steers-creators-away-from-short-lead-games`. [Playmore strings bundle](https://playmore.pl/assets/93jAIRDY.js) — read 2026-10-08

### Playtomic lets go of a court held by a match it thinks will not fill
- **[POLICY]** The staged hold (2 players more than 12 h ahead, 3 more than 4 h) "has been specifically designed by Playtomic to prevent club courts from being blocked by matches that are unlikely to be completed"; the player help says the system "predicts match completion based on time, club popularity, and player patterns". [Understanding When Open Matches Occupy Club Calendar Slots](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535700374929-Understanding-When-Open-Matches-Occupy-Club-Calendar-Slots), [Cancellation Policy for Open Matches](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831672824465-Cancellation-Policy-for-Open-Matches-Padel-Tennis) — read 2026-10-08

### Far-ahead yeses drop out more (outside sport)
- **[DOCS]** A systematic review of 105 studies of appointment no-shows found "high lead time and prior no-show history" the determinants reported most often. It is about clinics, not games. [Dantas et al., Health Policy 122(4):412–421, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.healthpol.2018.02.002) (abstract via [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/hepoli/v122y2018i4p412-421.html)) — read 2026-10-08

### No independent measure of fill by lead time
- **[ABSENT]** Any published fill rate of open games by how far ahead they were offered. Searched coverage of Playtomic's Global Padel Reports 2024–2026 (The Padel Paper, PADEL1969, Padel Magazine), Playtomic's manager help ("fill rate", "occupancy", "in advance"), and Meetup, Spond and OpenSports material — read 2026-10-08
