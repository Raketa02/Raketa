# Playtomic, seen by a newcomer

Topic `competitor-newcomer`: the main competitor, Playtomic, seen by a player new to the app and
to the local scene: signing up, level placement, a first game, a card, the first payment, the
first refund, and trust.

**Read on 2026-10-08** unless a line says otherwise. Lines dated 2026-10-06 or 2026-10-07 are
carried over, with their dates, from [`../../games-and-fill-in-market.md`](../../games-and-fill-in-market.md).
Playtomic's help pages return 403 to automated reads, so its articles were read through the help
centre's public API, which serves the same text. Links point at the pages a person opens (Zendesk
resolves `…/articles/<id>` without the title slug).

**Kinds:** `[POLICY]` the company's own help, terms, listing or page · `[PRICE]` a published price
· `[MEASURED]` seen by using the product, screenshot in `../shots/` · `[COMPLAINT]` one person's
account · `[ABSENT]` looked for and not found, with where.

## Read this first

- **A Kraków newcomer finds no Playtomic club.** The nearest listed are in Silesia, Opole and
  Wrocław (*Playtomic has no club in Kraków*).
- **Placement is one self-assessment, taken once:** about seven questions, a level from 0.5 to 5.7,
  one ±0.5 fix right afterwards. Raising it later takes wins, peer reviews or a paid coach session
  (*The questionnaire*, *Moving the level later*).
- **A new level is marked unreliable,** and some players avoid anyone below 50 % (*Reliability*,
  *Complaints about placement*).
- **A first game is with strangers inside a level range.** Out of range needs every seated player's
  yes; a casual match admits anyone (*The level range*).
- **The card is set up at the first checkout and charged at once,** plus a 20 % fee (1,24–3,99 zł).
  No BLIK, no Przelewy24 (*Price*, *No Polish methods*).
- **A refund takes 2–15 business days,** to the card that paid only (*How long a refund takes*).
- **Trust is split:** stores 4.7–4.8, Trustpilot 1.3; support is a chat bot with no Polish; the
  terms say the app is aimed at residents of Spain (*Store ratings and Trustpilot*, *The terms are
  written for Spain*).

## 3. Level placement

### The questionnaire

- "This initial assessment can only be completed once and gives you your starting level" (updated
  2026-10-07). **[POLICY]** [Help: How the level system works](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/43310980754193) — read 2026-10-08
- Needed "If you want to join open matches or competitions": "select your sport (Padel, Tennis, or

- One question per screen: a self-placement (Initiation / Intermediate / Advanced / Professional);
  "How many years have you been practicing padel or any racket sport?"; training in padel; "How old

### The result is bounded and can be fixed once

- "they can adjust it by 0.5 points, either up or down. The initial level cannot be lower than 0.5
  or higher than 5.7." "Playtomic will not change a user's level outside the aforementioned
  scenarios." **[POLICY]** [Legal Conditions §4 "Playtomic Level"](https://playtomic.com/legal-conditions) — read 2026-10-08
- The 0.5 fix is asked of Customer Care "immediately afterward". **[POLICY]** [How to create your account](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831938095633) — read 2026-10-08

### What the numbers mean

- "Less than 1 (Initiation): no or very little experience in racket sports · 1.0–1.49 (Beginner):
  still learning the basics · 1.5–2.4 (Initiation Intermediate) · 2.5–3.4 (Intermediate) · 3.5–4.4
  (Intermediate High) · 4.5–5.4 (Intermediate Advanced) · 5.4–5.6 (Competition)" (club help,
  updated 2026-10-07). The player help shows the chart only as an image. **[POLICY]** [Manager help: Levels & Algorithm](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20563641264145), [Help: Levels ups & downs](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831827459345) — read 2026-10-08

### Moving the level later

- Only competitive matches move it; casual never does. **[POLICY]** [How the level system works](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/43310980754193) — read 2026-10-08
- Lowering is padel-only and final: "you can't change it back". *Self-upgrade* (up to +0.5) unlocks
  after "a sufficient number of positive reviews from opponents", and vanishes if not used "before

- Paid levelling sessions sit under *Learn*, are paid in the app, need a questionnaire level first,
  and set "your reliability … to 50%" (updated 2026-10-07). Clubs run at most 4 per session and
  enter the result in "Column D ('new_level')" of a Google Sheet. **[POLICY]** [Help: Leveling Clubs](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19832024478097), [Manager help: Levelling Process](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/30903256332177) — read 2026-10-08
- A coach's adjustment "cannot be disputed by the user". **[POLICY]** [Legal Conditions §4](https://playtomic.com/legal-conditions) — read 2026-10-08
- The *10-Match Challenge* (2×–4× swings) needs "confidence level (85% or higher)": not for a
