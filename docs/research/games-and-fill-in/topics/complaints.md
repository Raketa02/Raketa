# Complaints — what organisers, joiners, newcomers and clubs say

Topic file of the Games & Fill in research (PRD #530). It decides nothing. Each complaint sits under a
`###` heading naming the behaviour complained about, with its side and the rule areas it touches, so a
grade can cite `topics/complaints.md#<heading>`.

**Read on 2026-10-08** unless a line says *read 2026-10-06*: those lines are carried over, with their
links and dates, from [the market file §5](../../games-and-fill-in-market.md#5-complaints).

**Sources read on 2026-10-08:** the App Store's own review feeds, newest first, up to 500 a store —
Playtomic in PL, GB, ES, IT, FR, DE, NL, SE, PT, IE, BE; Playmore (Reservise), tenis4U, Kluby.org, Pado
in PL; MATCHi in SE, DK, NO, GB; Padel Mates in GB, SE (2,425 reviews). Google Play, newest first, for
Playtomic, Playmore, Kluby.org, tenis4U, Pado, in Polish and in English as the Polish store serves them
(1,077); the English ones come from anywhere and are marked *GP-en*. Trustpilot (Playtomic page 4), OCU's
public complaints (Spain), The Padel Paper.

Quotes are verbatim, cut with "…"; [brackets] translate. A line is **[COMPLAINT]** (one person's
account) unless tagged **[DATA]**, **[POLICY]** (a company's own words) or **[ABSENT]** (looked for,
not found). Dates are the review's date; a country is the store's.
