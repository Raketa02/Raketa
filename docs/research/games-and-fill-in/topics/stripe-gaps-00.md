### R-PLATFORM-5, R-PLATFORM-20 — a hold or refund on a club Rakieta removed, or whose charges Stripe disabled

**Answered for a removed club. `[ABSENT]` for disabled charges.**

#### A removed club

- **[DOCS]** "You can disconnect a connected account from its account details page in your Dashboard. Click the
  overflow menu (⋯) … then select **Remove account**. This action disconnects the account from your platform and
  revokes access in the same way as the OAuth de-authorize endpoint. Removing an account from your platform also
  permanently resets all platform controls on the account, such as payout timing, even if the account reconnects
  later. **It doesn't otherwise affect the account, which still functions as a normal Stripe account.**"
  [Platform controls — Remove accounts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/platform-controls-for-stripe-dashboard-accounts)
  — 2026-10-20.
- **[DOCS]** "Only accounts where your platform is liable for negative account balances, which includes Custom and
  Express accounts, can be rejected." A rejection "Returns an account with `payouts_enabled` and `charges_enabled`
  set to false".
  [Reject an account](https://docs.stripe.com/api/account/reject) — 2026-10-20.
  - Since `2026-07-29.dahlia`, `payouts_action` (`pause` or `none`) lets a rejection leave payouts running.
    [Changelog](https://docs.stripe.com/changelog/dahlia/2026-07-29/reject-connected-accounts-with-non-zero-balances)
    — 2026-10-20.
- **[MEASURED]** read via the SDK in the copy, 2026-10-20: every club account opened during the runs, and the test
  club, reads `controller.losses.payments: stripe`, `controller.fees.payer: account` and

  So **Rakieta cannot reject a club**. Its only removal is the disconnect quoted above.
- **Carried:** after a disconnect, Rakieta's key fails for that club (`platform_api_key_expired`).
  [`stripe-connect.md#a-disconnected-club-makes-rakietas-key-fail-for-it`](stripe-connect.md#a-disconnected-club-makes-rakietas-key-fail-for-it)

  - **The club** can still take or let go a hold, and refund a taken share, from its own full Dashboard.

### R-PLATFORM-66 — whether a club on per-card (IC+) or other custom pricing gets a fee on the charge's balance transaction, whether `charge.updated` fires, and standalone fees or fee credits

**Answered in substance.**

- **On IC+, the balance transaction carries no usable payment fee.**
- **A club on standalone fees shows a fee of 0,00 zł there, and the real fee comes later in a separate,
  day-aggregated transaction.**
- **Accounts move to standalone fees for custom pricing or fee credits.**
- Still `[ABSENT]`: whether `charge.updated` fires for these clubs, and whether a new Polish club can be moved.

The facts:

- **[DOCS]** "**IC+ users can't retrieve payment fee information from the balance transaction.** Use the Payment
  fees report instead." Also: "The charge.updated event references the `balance_transaction` property … indicating
  that it's ready to use."
  [Use cases for expanding responses](https://docs.stripe.com/expand/use-cases?locale=en-GB) — 2026-10-20.
- **[DOCS]** Standalone fees, all from [Standalone fees](https://docs.stripe.com/standalone-fees), 2026-10-20:
  - "**New Stripe accounts default to inline fees, but might transition to standalone fees** for more flexibility in
    fee application. This change can occur for reasons such as **custom pricing (for example, tiered pricing) or
    Stripe fee credit awards**."
  - "We create a single balance transaction for each transaction that only includes the total charge amount, **with
    fees set to zero**. Within 36 hours of the charge, we create a second fee-specific balance transaction with the
    *aggregate fees* of all transactions processed within that period."
  - The Dashboard's per-payment fee "might take up to 2 hours … While the fee is being calculated, it shows as
    'pending'".
  - "Fees for transactions are separated from the transaction and reported in aggregate per product at the end of
    the day. The aggregated amounts are accurate, but they aren't tied to the individual transactions."
- **[DOCS]** "Fee data becomes available within 96 hours after a fee affects your balance." To find a charge's fees,
  filter the itemised report's `incurred_by` by the charge ID, with the incurred-at basis, which "includes
  standalone fees that Stripe applies after the transaction".
  [Fees report](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/all-fees) — 2026-10-20.
- **[DOCS]** "Card networks can bill fees for a given payment up to two months after it was processed."
  [Reviewing IC+ fees](https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-ic-fees) — 2026-10-20.
- **[DOCS]** Fee credits appear as balance transactions of type `fee_credit_funding`: "When a credit is created, you
  can see the balance that was created with this event."
  [Balance transaction types](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/balance-transaction-types) — 2026-10-20.
  The Fees report's `settled_via` says how each fee was paid: `credit`, `balance`, `v2_balance`, `invoice` or
  `multiple` ([Fees report](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/all-fees)). A fee paid from a credit takes nothing from
  the club's balance.
- **[MEASURED]** read via the SDK in the copy, 2026-10-20:
  - **The test club is on inline fees.** A 40,00 zł share's balance transaction carries 2,30 zł at the capture
    (carried, [`stripe-sandbox.md#stripes-fee-on-a-share-230-zł-on-40-zł-with-stripes-us-test-card`](stripe-sandbox.md#stripes-fee-on-a-share-230-zł-on-40-zł-with-stripes-us-test-card)).
    The same holds for the 7726 and 5126 shares read today: `fee 230`, `pending`, available on 2026-10-26.
  - The club's 30 days of events hold **no `charge.updated`** after 56 manual captures.
  - On another sandbox account, `acct_1US8XX5y1Dlh7WsG`, an `automatic_async` payment of 100,00 zł
    (`ch_3US8Xf5y1Dlh7WsG0Z91Ib5q`, 04:51:24) produced `charge.updated` three seconds later. Its
    `previous_attributes` had `balance_transaction: null`. So the event does announce the balance transaction when
    it is attached late.
- **Reading, against the code:**
  - `_fee_of` takes the balance transaction's `fee` whenever it is a whole number in złoty
    (`backend/app/modules/payments/infrastructure/stripe_payments.py:115-127`). A standalone-fee club's `fee: 0` is
    a whole number. So it is recorded as a **known fee of 0,00 zł**, not as "not yet known"
    (`backend/app/modules/payments/domain/seat_charge.py:446-459`, `:756-790`).
  - **The late leaver's refund would then give back the whole 40,00 zł.** The club bears the card fee that Stripe
    takes up to 36 hours later in an aggregate transaction no share points to. Rakieta's net for the club would be
    over by that fee.
  - The code's own comment expects such a club's fee to arrive "later" as `None`, then a report. The documented
    shape is `0`, then a separate aggregate. *Unverified on a live club.*
- **[ABSENT]** Looked in the five pages above and a docs search, 2026-10-20. **A question for Stripe support:**
  - whether `charge.updated` fires for an IC+ or standalone-fee charge when its fee becomes known;
