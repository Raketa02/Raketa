# NOTIFY — notices, pushes, the bell and the caps: market digest

Rules R-NOTIFY-1 to R-NOTIFY-31: which notices are always on and which are a choice, asking the phone,
pushes that don't arrive, the list behind the bell and the banner, what a notice says, the daily caps,
and the no-show mark nobody is told about. R-NOTIFY-9 to R-NOTIFY-11 (how phones are registered) are
internal and no market fact touches them. Written 2026-10-08 from the topic files (read 2026-10-06 to
2026-10-08). Paths are relative to this folder; `market.md` means `../games-and-fill-in-market.md`. Kinds
as in the topics; *reading* is comparison, not a source.

**Read this first.** Playtomic's emails (confirmation, receipt, cancellation) "cannot be disabled"; its pushes are social activity, contacts' matches and one free priority alert, and nothing tells a player that someone joined or left their match — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#on-joining-a-confirmation-email-and-the-clubs-message`, `topics/competitor-organiser.md#the-organiser-is-not-told-when-someone-joins-or-leaves-an-open-match`. Playmore tells players "Ktoś dołączył, gra potwierdzona, gra anulowana" and runs waiting lists that alert everyone at once — `topics/platforms.md#playmore--waiting-lists-and-alerts`.

- **Competitor (R-NOTIFY-1, -3).** [POLICY] Emails for "Confirmation of new booking, match or activity joined", "Receipt of a transaction", "Cancellation of a booking, match or activity"; "E-mail notifications cannot be disabled" — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#on-joining-a-confirmation-email-and-the-clubs-message`.

- **Competitor (R-NOTIFY-1).** [POLICY] A manager's chat message pushes to every player; "Users always receive emails and in-app notifications about any changes to their reservations" — `topics/competitor-club.md#talking-to-players-and-what-the-club-sees-of-them`, `topics/competitor-club.md#what-a-manager-may-and-may-not-change`.

- **Platforms (R-NOTIFY-1, -28).** [POLICY] MATCHi's auto-cancel refunds everyone "with a notification", and the customer gets a cancellation email whoever cancels — `topics/platforms.md#matchi--venue-matches-pay-at-join-optional-auto-cancel`, `market.md#s02-g3--blocking-courts-for-a-tournament-or-repair-on-tenis4u-klubyorg-playmore-matchi`.
