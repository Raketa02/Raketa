## 1. The club is the seller; Raketa takes no fee (R-PAY-1, 41)

- **Playtomic:** under its current model "payments are now processed directly between the player and your club", net of a per-contract commission `CC#nbm-the-club-sells-and-is-paid-weekly-by-payment-date-before-the-game` `CC#a-commission-on-every-online-payment-per-contract`
- **Playtomic:** the player adds a fee on an open-match spot (20 %, 1,24–3,99 zł: 43,99 zł for a 40 zł spot), refunded if she leaves in time `CJ#the-polish-service-fee-on-an-open-match-spot`
- **Playtomic:** checkout names the club by trading name, never a legal name or tax number `CN#who-sells-and-what-she-is-sent`
- **Other platforms:** Playmore: "zawierasz umowę bezpośrednio z Partnerem", but the money passes through Reservise; 1,48 zł player fee at Atras; clubs pay 1,79 % `PF#playmore--operator-scale-and-seller` `PF#playmore--the-clubs-money-passes-through-reservise` `PF#playmore--player-fees` `PF#playmore--what-a-club-pays`
- **Other platforms:** tenis4U and kluby.org are "not a party" (kluby.org keeps 2 % online); PADO is not merchant of record and takes 2 % `PF#tenis4u--payment-and-the-2--refund-deduction` `PF#klubyorg--payments-partial-prepayment-and-its-fee` `PF#pado--seller-and-payments`
- **Stripe:** a direct charge makes the club merchant of record; flow, terms and receipt must say who sells, and the club's descriptor shows on the statement `LM#direct-charges-make-the-club-the-seller`
- **Stripe:** the club pays the fees (1,5 % + 1 zł on standard EEA cards, up to 3,25 % + 1 zł international), not returned on a refund `LM#what-polish-operators-charge-against-stripe` `LM#who-pays-stripes-fees`
- **Law:** before she is bound the player sees the seller's identity and address, the total price and how payment is taken (art. 12) `LM#what-the-player-must-see-before-being-bound`
- **Status quo (club):** the club is the seller in tenis4U and kluby.org; an online refund keeps a flat 2 % `LM#the-club-is-the-seller-the-platform-is-not-a-party` `LM#the-refund-deduction-players-already-know-a-flat-2-`
- **Complaints (joiner):** "Dlaczego mam płacić prowizję… kiedy jest to jedyna możliwa opcja"; Playmore "przerzucanie opłat transakcyjnych na klienta" `CP#a-player-fee-on-top-of-the-court-price`
- **Situations:** a tourist's card costs the club twice the fee (SIT-58); the statement names the club, and "Unrecognized" is a dispute (SIT-61) `SIT#paying`

## 2. Nothing moves until the game fills; then each share is held and taken on the club's account (R-PAY-2, 3, 34)

- **Playtomic:** open-match spots are "charged immediately, regardless of the country or model", the organiser's first `CJ#she-pays-her-own-spot-online-at-join-charged-at-once` `CO#what-the-creator-pays-the-spot-and-the-service-fee`
- **Playtomic:** split private bookings: a hold of the full court in some countries; in Poland unpaid shares most likely become the owner's debt `CO#who-covers-unpaid-shares-three-guarantees-by-country`
- **Other platforms:** Playmore charges at join; MATCHi holds the card, captures 12 h after the start or 7 days after booking; kluby.org charges the booker for unpaid shares 2 h after `PF#playmore--open-games-each-player-pays-at-join` `PF#matchi--the-card-is-held-at-booking-and-captured-later` `PF#klubyorg--payments-partial-prepayment-and-its-fee`
- **Stripe:** save once, then clone the card to the club's account for each charge; one charge spends a clone `LM#one-saved-card-charged-on-each-clubs-account`
- **Stripe:** a hold lasts 4 days 18 h (Visa, merchant-initiated) to 7 days; cancelling it costs nothing; BLIK and P24 cannot be held `LM#how-long-a-hold-lasts`
- **Law:** a charge without the player needs a recorded agreement (when, how much, cancellation policy); the order button must say it obliges payment, even if payment waits on a condition `LM#charging-a-saved-card-without-the-player-there` `LM#the-order-button-and-a-yes-that-charges-later`
- **Law:** new terms reach a later automatic charge "only with the prior consent of the consumer"; the payer may reclaim an unexpected payee-initiated charge within 8 weeks `LM#which-terms-bind-when-a-yes-becomes-a-booking-later` `LM#the-payers-8-week-refund-right-on-a-charge-they-didnt-trigger`
- **Status quo:** clubs take prepayment by P24 or at the desk; the organiser collects by free BLIK transfer or one request to several friends `LM#paying-ahead-and-online-is-cheaper-than-the-desk` `LM#paying-a-friend-back-blik-to-a-phone-number-and-a-payment-request`
- **Complaints (organiser):** "it now charges you the full 60 and refunds you within 2 weeks"; holds that "stay on your account for up to a week" `CP#the-organisers-card-carries-everyones-share-until-the-others-pay`
- **Situations:** a hold runs out (SIT-63); the bank is down at night (SIT-30); the share exceeds the card's online limit (SIT-70) `SIT#paying` `SIT#time-and-the-calendar`

## 3. What can be saved: cards and the phone's wallet, BLIK waiting (R-PAY-4, 5, 6, 7)

- **Playtomic:** card, Apple Pay, Google Pay (sometimes on request) and Club Wallet; no BLIK or Przelewy24 `CJ#ways-to-pay-in-poland-card-apple-pay-google-pay-club-wallet-no-blik` `CC#payment-methods-no-blik-no-przelewy24`
- **Other platforms:** Playmore names BLIK at join; PADO lists BLIK, Apple Pay and Google Pay; tenis4U and kluby.org take P24; MATCHi has no Polish method `PF#playmore--open-games-each-player-pays-at-join` `PF#pado--seller-and-payments` `PF#matchi--scale-owner-and-seller`
- **Stripe, BLIK:** no manual capture; a saved BLIK charges only off-session, up to 2 000 zł, at nine banks, and cannot be cloned to a club's account; the RN setup sheet saves only cards and PayPal `LM#blik-at-stripe`
- **Stripe, P24:** single-use only, no saving `LM#przelewy24-at-stripe`; no Polish court platform saves BLIK [ABSENT] `LM#recurring-blik-at-the-banks`
- **Stripe, wallets:** an Apple Pay token is tied to the device and can fail later charges; merchant tokens exist, but no page covers one unscheduled later charge [ABSENT] `LM#apple-pay-and-google-pay-for-a-later-charge`
- **Status quo:** BLIK is half of remote payments, cards a quarter; online shoppers use BLIK 64 %, card 37 %, Google Pay 14 %, Apple Pay 10 % `LM#how-poles-pay-online--cards-are-a-minority`
- **Complaints (joiner):** "lack of possibility in payment with BLIK is disappointing"; Playmore's Google Pay "wymaga logowania się do banku" `CP#no-blik-no-google-pay-no-paypal`
- **Situations:** Apple Pay breaks on a new phone (SIT-69); a single-use virtual card is dead by the next game (SIT-68) `SIT#paying`

## 4. Saving a card: the terms first, then the bank (R-PAY-8, 10 to 13, 16, 17)

- **Playtomic:** the card is added at the first checkout in four screens, "Save the payment method" pre-ticked, no terms tick-box `CJ#adding-a-card-at-checkout-takes-four-screens` `CN#adding-the-first-card`
- **Playtomic:** 3-D Secure is mentioned nowhere [ABSENT] `CJ#3-d-secure-and-failed-payments`
- **Playtomic (club):** a club may require its own versioned terms; each edit makes players accept again, stored with a timestamp `CC#the-clubs-own-terms-versioned-at-checkout`
- **Playtomic:** checkout shows a cancellation-policy row but never warns that the free window has passed [ABSENT] `CJ#the-checkout-shows-the-cancellation-block-never-a-passed-deadline`
- **Other platforms:** PADO's provider "must display" its cancellation rule before payment; PADO and Playmore state that a dated booking has no withdrawal right, Playtomic is silent `PF#pado--fees-cancellation-and-disputes` `LM#no-14-day-withdrawal-for-a-dated-court-booking`
- **Law:** confirmation on a durable medium before the service (art. 21); keeping more than actual costs on a cancelled service is presumed abusive `LM#what-the-player-must-see-before-being-bound` `LM#cancellation-fees-and-abusive-clauses`
- **Complaints:** a saved card "not offered when booking"; cards that cannot be added for months `CP#the-saved-card-is-not-offered-or-is-declined-with-no-reason` `CN#when-the-first-payment-fails`

## 9. The Payments screen: live yeses, receipts (R-PAY-9, 30, 36 to 38, 40, 42)

- **Playtomic:** payments are listed under *Your payments*; the receipt is "Get the invoice by email" or only the fee's receipt, by club set-up `CN#how-long-a-refund-takes` `CJ#the-receipt-is-the-full-share-or-only-the-fee`
- **Playtomic:** "not easy to find my next match. Would be great to see it right on the main screen" `CJ#finding-your-own-next-game`
- **Stripe:** direct-charge receipts use the club's settings and must show its legal name, support address and privacy policy `LM#receipts-on-a-direct-charge-come-from-the-clubs-stripe-settings`
- **Law:** no cash register for a court paid fully online when each payment is traceable to its sale; an emailed confirmation sufficed in a ruling `LM#no-cash-register-for-sales-paid-fully-online`
- **Law:** a consumer gets a VAT invoice on request within 3 months; court time carries 8 % VAT; Playtomic offers nothing for Poland `LM#invoices-on-request-for-consumers-always-for-businesses` `LM#vat-on-court-time-is-8-` `LM#playtomic-offers-nothing-for-polish-fiscal-rules`
- **Complaints:** "you can't get receipts from the club"; "App does not produce VAT receipt" `CP#no-receipt-or-vat-invoice`

## 10. The refund and the card fee kept (R-PAY-40, 41)

- **Playtomic:** leaving in time refunds everything, fee included; a card refund "can take 2-15 business days", a same-day one is a reversal `CJ#leaving-refunds-in-full-the-service-fee-included` `CJ#refunds-go-back-to-the-card-in-days-to-a-wallet-at-once`
- **Other platforms:** Playmore refunds to its wallet and keeps the fee; MATCHi refunds the spot minus its fee before the cut-off `PF#playmore--refunds-go-to-the-wallet-and-the-fee-is-kept` `PF#matchi--venue-matches-pay-at-join-optional-auto-cancel`
- **Stripe:** card refunds land in about 5–10 business days; a refund comes out of the club's balance and waits if it is short `LM#how-fast-a-refund-arrives-by-method` `LM#refunds-come-out-of-the-clubs-balance`
- **Status quo:** players know a flat 2 % kept (0,80 zł on 40 zł), against Stripe's 1,60 zł `LM#the-refund-deduction-players-already-know-a-flat-2-` `LM#what-polish-operators-charge-against-stripe`
- **Complaints:** "Cancelo… más de 24 horas antes… me penalizan con 1,50€"; refunds slow or never seen; refunds to a wallet, not the card `CP#leaving-costs-a-fee-even-before-the-deadline` `CP#refunds-are-slow-partial-or-never-arrive` `CP#a-refund-lands-in-a-club-wallet-not-on-the-card`
- **Situation:** a refund to a closed card can bounce back to the seller up to 30 days later (SIT-65) `SIT#paying`
