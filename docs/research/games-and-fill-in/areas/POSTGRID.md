## 1. The way in, and what the poster chooses (R-POSTGRID-1, R-POSTGRID-3, R-POSTGRID-39)

- **Competitor (R-POSTGRID-1).** [POLICY] Two doors: *Find a match* → sport → where → when → "opening one yourself", or a club's *Open Matches* tab → a slot → "Create a new match"; an empty search offers "Be the first player!" — `topics/competitor-organiser.md#creating-an-open-match-the-doors-and-what-the-creator-sets`; a floating "+ Start a match" sits under the list — `market.md#s05-g-1--how-a-playtomic-open-match-shows-its-players-price-gender-and-type`.
- **Competitor (R-POSTGRID-39).** [POLICY] The creator sets type, gender and, through their own level, the range; the court is "Autoselect"; one partner may be added at creation only; others come by a shared link, followers' push or Premium "Golden Matches" — `topics/competitor-organiser.md#creating-an-open-match-the-doors-and-what-the-creator-sets`, `topics/competitor-organiser.md#sharing-and-promoting-an-open-match`.
- **Platforms (R-POSTGRID-39).** [POLICY] Playmore's organiser picks a free court and hour, then invites by WhatsApp, "Zaproś Buddies" or a link; PADO players "send or accept an invite" — `topics/platforms.md#playmore--invites-by-whatsapp-and-a-clubs-community-links`, `topics/platforms.md#pado--matchmaking-auto-fill-and-elo`.
- **Status quo.** [POLICY] In Kraków players arrange games over WhatsApp, and whoever books owes the whole court — `topics/local-market.md#arranging-by-whatsapp-a-kraków-padel-league`, `topics/local-market.md#the-booker-owes-the-whole-court`.
- **Complaints (organiser).** [COMPLAINT] "the organizers cannot manage the participants at the match" (GB, 2025-08-22); "why don't I have control of who I play against" (GB, 2026-08-14) — `topics/complaints.md#the-organiser-cannot-manage-who-is-in-the-game`, `topics/complaints.md#shared-or-booked-private-by-mistake-and-it-cannot-be-undone`.

- **Competitor (R-POSTGRID-4, R-POSTGRID-5).** [POLICY] Open matches are padel and tennis only, and a level from a questionnaire per sport comes first — `topics/competitor-organiser.md#two-ways-to-organise-a-private-booking-or-an-open-match`, `topics/competitor-joiner.md#an-account-a-level-and-a-public-profile-are-needed-to-join`.

- **Platforms (R-POSTGRID-4).** [POLICY] Playmore shows counts, not names: "{count} osób szuka grania w tym klubie" — `market.md#s05-g-8--published-weekly-availability-a-groups-shared-free-hours`.

## 3. Who: Anyone, Men, Women, Mixed pairs (R-POSTGRID-9 to R-POSTGRID-11)

- **Competitor.** [POLICY] All Players (the default), Only Women, Only Men or Mixed ("Each team consists of a man and a woman"), fixed by the first player; a player of the wrong gender "will be unable to participate" — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#the-matchs-gender-is-checked-at-join`, `market.md#75-rankings-notices-blocks-preferences-limits-and-dispute-codes-s04-s06-s07-s08-gaps`.
- **Competitor and platforms (R-POSTGRID-10).** [POLICY] Gender is optional on Playtomic, and [ABSENT] what it does with none set; MATCHi filters "only … when your profile gender is set to male or female"; [DATA] Padel Mates turns an unset gender away — `market.md#s03-g16--does-playtomic-require-a-players-gender-and-how-are-women-only-matches-enforced`.
- **Situations (R-POSTGRID-9).** [POLICY] No more than two on a squash court at once (AGH Kraków) — `situations.md` SIT-80.
- **Complaints.** [COMPLAINT] "if three females are already in the game, then a guy joins… we need to contact the venue" (GB) — `topics/complaints.md#women-only-games-and-keeping-an-unwanted-player-out`.

## 4. The level range (R-POSTGRID-12 to R-POSTGRID-14)

- **Competitor.** [POLICY] A player-made competitive range is set automatically, "-0.25 / +0.75" around the first player, 1.0 wide on 0–7; a Friendly match is 0–7 — `topics/competitor-organiser.md#the-level-range-follows-the-creator`.
- **Competitor (R-POSTGRID-13).** [POLICY] Out of range she may "Request a spot"; "it is enough for one player to reject" — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#requesting-a-spot-needs-every-players-yes-and-holds-no-seat`.
- **Competitor (R-POSTGRID-12).** [POLICY] A level shows its reliability ("Level 2.04 · Level reliability: 15% LOW") — `market.md#s05-g-13--what-a-playtomic-player-profile-shows-when-opened-from-a-match`.
- **Platforms (R-POSTGRID-12 to R-POSTGRID-14).** [POLICY] Playmore proposes a band from the creator's score, "możesz zmienić"; MATCHi's slider range "endast är vägledande", while its venue matches refuse outsiders ("This match requires skill level X–Y") — `market.md#s04-g5--how-wide-is-a-player-created-competitive-range-on-playtomic-what-spread-do-casual-matches-accept`, `topics/platforms.md#matchi--venue-matches-pay-at-join-optional-auto-cancel`.
- **Status quo.** [POLICY] The Kraków league places unranked players by self-evaluation over WhatsApp — `topics/local-market.md#arranging-by-whatsapp-a-kraków-padel-league`.
- **Complaints.** [COMPLAINT] "Si abres un partido el rango siempre es mayor… acabas saliéndote del partido que tú mismo has abierto" (ES); "Es desesperante no poder entrar a los partidos por tu nivel" (ES) — `topics/complaints.md#the-other-players-are-not-at-the-level-shown`, `topics/complaints.md#shut-out-by-level-ranges-and-by-friends-private-games`.

- **Competitor (R-POSTGRID-17).** [POLICY] Nothing shows who is free when; availability is a profile drop-down ("Preferred time to play — All day") — `market.md#s05-g-8--published-weekly-availability-a-groups-shared-free-hours`.
- **Platforms (R-POSTGRID-17, R-POSTGRID-19, R-POSTGRID-22).** [POLICY] Playmore *Szukam grania*: "Kiedy zwykle możesz grać? — To nie rezerwacja"; seekers see "Wspólne terminy: …"; *Challenger* shows rivals a weekly grid — `topics/platforms.md#playmore--weekly-availability-grids`.

## Gaps round answers (2026-10-08)

- **R-POSTGRID-3 [POLICY]/[ABSENT]:** Playmore's creator resets every time it opens and keeps no draft. Only a one-tap "Załóż jak ostatnio" copies the last game the player created. Playtomic describes no draft [g3a] [g3b].
- **R-POSTGRID-10 [POLICY]/[ABSENT]:** Playtomic sets gender on the match and checks joiners against it, gender is optional, and what happens with no gender is unstated. Padel Mates keeps "other" out of single-gender games. Playmore has no adult gender. AUN and Padeligo only filter. No app honours a player's own preference both ways [g10a] [g10b] [g10c] [g10d].
- **R-POSTGRID-19, R-POSTGRID-17 [POLICY]/[ABSENT]:** Playmore's creator counts matching seekers for the one slot picked ("{count} pasujące osoby dostaną powiadomienie"). Its singles ladder shows, hour by hour, who in range can play, with the court checked. Nothing checks that four players fit each other [g19a] [g19b].
- **R-POSTGRID-43 [POLICY]/[ABSENT]:** After "Ktoś właśnie zajął ten termin", Playmore returns the creator to step 1 and re-reads the free slots. Playtomic describes no clash [g43a] [g43b].
- **R-POSTGRID-41 [POLICY]:** Playmore's create request sends no price. The organiser pays on the next screen, which refuses a changed total with "Cena tego terminu się zmieniła. Odśwież i sprawdź nową cenę." [g41].
- **R-POSTGRID-45 [POLICY]/[ABSENT]:** Playmore's creator always shows "Do {hours} h przed startem wypiszesz się ze zwrotem…", whatever the time to the start. Its court checkout does say "Termin bezpłatnego odwołania minął" [g45].
- **R-POSTGRID-15 [POLICY]/[MEASURED]:** On tenis4U, seven Kraków padel clubs book 14 days ahead, Fame and Squash&Padel 30, and Padel SPOT 7. kluby.org's Padel House schedule stops at 7. Playmore's three Kraków squash clubs book 14, and its creator follows that window, opening at T+3 [g15a] [g15b] [g15c].
- **R-POSTGRID-28 [POLICY]:** Playmore's ladder sets hours from a player's past matches and shows them to rivals "przy Twoim nazwisku" before she confirms them. Its seeker list stays two-way opt-in [g28].
- **R-POSTGRID-9, R-POSTGRID-11 [POLICY]/[ABSENT]:** On Playtomic, "Only women can create and join" a women's match. Mixed means a man and a woman per team ("for pair leagues"), and Mixed on a single court is undocumented [g9a] [g9b].
- **R-POSTGRID-34, R-POSTGRID-36 [LAW]/[ABSENT]:** The Polish Language Act arts. 7, 7a and 8 require Polish for a Kraków consumer's offer, consumer information and contract, with English allowed only alongside it. German courts held "Jetzt anmelden" too weak as a paying button. No ruling on "I'm in" [g34a] [g34b].
