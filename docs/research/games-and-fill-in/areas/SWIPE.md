a yes. Digest written 2026-10-08 from the topic files (read 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-08). Paths are

## 1. A yes before the last seat (R-SWIPE-1, R-SWIPE-2, R-SWIPE-14, R-SWIPE-15, R-SWIPE-21, R-SWIPE-27, R-SWIPE-28, R-SWIPE-32)

- **Competitor.** [POLICY] Joining a Playtomic open match is paying: the spot is charged at once — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#she-pays-her-own-spot-online-at-join-charged-at-once`; [PRICE] the 20 % fee is capped at 3,99 zł, so a 40 zł spot costs 43,99 zł — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#the-polish-service-fee-on-an-open-match-spot`.
- **Competitor (R-SWIPE-1, R-SWIPE-15).** [ABSENT] No push or email when someone joins or leaves an open match; only a Community Match's creator is told — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#players-are-not-told-when-someone-joins-or-leaves-an-open-match`.
- **Platforms (R-SWIPE-15).** [POLICY] Playmore tells players "Ktoś dołączył, gra potwierdzona, gra anulowana" — `topics/platforms.md#playmore--waiting-lists-and-alerts`.
- **Platforms (R-SWIPE-27).** [POLICY] Racket swipe decks deal people with a mutual yes, with ✕ and ✓ buttons (Noavo "Przesuń. Dopasuj. Graj."); none charges on a swipe — `topics/platforms.md#swipe-decks-deal-people-not-games`, `market.md#s05-g-11--swipeable-card-decks-a-swipe-or-one-tap-that-pays-padel-mates-suggestions`.
- **Law (R-SWIPE-1, R-SWIPE-32).** [LAW] A control that binds a consumer to pay must say so, even when payment falls due only after a later condition (CJEU *Conny*); [ABSENT] no authority on a swipe or "I'm in" — `topics/local-market.md#the-order-button-and-a-yes-that-charges-later`.
- **Cap (R-SWIPE-21).** [POLICY] Caps are the club's: Playmore "maks. {max} aktywne gry naraz", Padel Mates' per-club join limit, PADELHUB's active-booking cap — `topics/platforms.md#playmore--the-clubs-open-game-settings`, `topics/platforms.md#padel-mates--find-a-replacement-for-your-spot`, `situations.md` SIT-49.
- **Complaints (R-SWIPE-21).** [COMPLAINT] Playtomic: "unless you pay, you are restricted to only 2 games registered on the app at any one time" — `topics/competitor-newcomer.md#how-many-games-at-once`.
- **Status quo (R-SWIPE-14).** [POLICY] WhatsApp events: "others can respond so the whole group knows who is coming" — `topics/platforms.md#whatsapp-groups-polls-events-and-1024-members`; a Kraków meetup takes sign-ups by a Register button — `topics/local-market.md#a-meetup-splits-the-court-among-whoever-comes`.
- *reading:* a Playtomic joiner pays at join; a Raketa yes moves no money until the last seat — `topics/competitor-joiner.md#reading-on-money-at-the-moment-of-joining`.

## 2. What may refuse a yes (R-SWIPE-3 to R-SWIPE-8, R-SWIPE-20, R-SWIPE-22 to R-SWIPE-25)

- **Situations.** Two people who fell out (SIT-92); a player who keeps not showing (SIT-50) — `situations.md`.
- **Complaints (R-SWIPE-19, R-SWIPE-20).** [COMPLAINT] "donde está la opción de blacklist?"; a man joins three women and "we need to contact the venue to get him removed" — `topics/complaints.md#women-only-games-and-keeping-an-unwanted-player-out`; "the organizers cannot manage the participants at the match" — `topics/complaints.md#the-organiser-cannot-manage-who-is-in-the-game`.
