# RESCUE — Fill in: seats to fill, taking a seat, You're In and Withdraw

Digest for R-RESCUE-1 to 40, 2026-10-08; no new facts. Each line cites the topic section holding the link and read date. Playtomic has no Kraków club [j-krakow].

## 1. Where a freed seat shows (R-RESCUE-1, 2, 3, 7, 8)

- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** a seat freed before the 24 h line reopens in *Find a match* to anyone in range; no waiting list, queue, transfer or offer to the next in 94 articles [j-no-waitlist] [o-refill]. Inside 24 h of a full match the seat cannot be released at all [j-late-club].
- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** the card shows no spot count and no fill-by time [j-no-deadline].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore's card: "2/4 · brakuje 2 graczy" / "Ostatnie miejsce", and "Komplet do {time} · Zostało {h} godz. {m} min", refreshed every 30 s [p-card]; Planeta Padla: "Brakuje 1 osoby · dziś 20:00 · 3.0–3.5 · 3/4 graczy · Dołącz", and a "Giełda terminów: oddaj albo przejmij rezerwację" [planeta]; kluby.org posts cancelled bookings to its portal automatically [k-pause].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** Squash4You's desk tries to resell a late-cancelled court [l-cutoff]; in WhatsApp groups the organiser chases a fourth, "szukamy czwartego" [SIT-93].
- **Complaints:** a late seat "cannot be released… we could easily find another player within a few hours" [c-dropped]; "Why do we have to waste our time chatting and checking who frees the spot instead of a button click?!" [j-queue].

## 2. Who a seat is offered to (R-RESCUE-4, 5, 6, 9, 10)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** whoever joins must fit the match: a blocked player cannot join [j-blocked]; the first player's gender setting is checked and fixed [j-gender]; a competitive match admits only players in range, others may request [j-range].
- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** whether a leaver may rejoin [j-rejoin].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore: "Zapisz się na rezerwę… powiadomimy wszystkich z listy naraz — kto pierwszy, ten gra" [p-waitlist]; MATCHi and Eversports notify everyone at once and enrol nobody [m-wait] [ev-wait]; PADO's club CRM has "Auto-Fill Matches" [pado-fill]; Padel Mates lets the leaver pick a replacement by link [pm-replace].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** only small apps show a balance figure (Padel Pals "Win probability 59% / 41%") [balance].
- **Complaints:** a queue asked for: "pierwsza osoba z kolejki ma opcję się zapisać np przez 15 min, potem kolejna" [c-waitlist]; a seat announced in a "She serves group which means men don't get notified" [c-chasing]; a man joined three women and the venue had to remove him [c-women].

## 3. The rescue from outside (R-RESCUE-11, 12, 13)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** the match page shows "✓ Court booked", sides A and B, *Calendar*, *Chat*, *Join match* [j-match-page]; the chat is open to anyone, no spot needed [j-chat-public]; the card shows each seat's level and the price per spot [j-card].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore's preview before "Dołącz za {price}": roster, note, and three rules (only if full, the call-off hour, the free-leave window) [p-card].
- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a requested spot is not held: "another player could take yours before you finish booking" [j-request].

## 4. I'm in: paying at once, the Undo, a lost race (R-RESCUE-14 to 19, 33, 34)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a joiner pays her spot at join, charged at once [j-pay-join], at a price that can differ by the court free then [j-price-differs], plus 20 % capped at 3,99 zł (40 zł → 43,99 zł) [j-fee]; a first card takes four screens at checkout [j-add-card].
- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** 3-D Secure, a retry screen, or a seat held while a payment fails [j-3ds].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore names a lost race: "Ktoś właśnie zajął ten termin. Wybierz inny slot." [p-held]; no app found where a swipe or one tap pays for a spot [swipe].
- **Law [LAW]:** the button that binds must say payment is owed ("zamówienie z obowiązkiem zapłaty" or equal) or no contract forms; no ruling on "I'm in" [l-button]; before binding the player must see the seller's identity, the total and how payment is taken [l-must-see].
- **Complaints:** charged twice, or charged though the booking failed [c-charged]; the saved card not offered, or a "Generic Decline" [c-card].

## 5. The leaver's refund when the seat is taken (R-RESCUE-20, 21, 22)

- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Padel Mates: "If someone joins using this link, you will be automatically removed from the activity and receive a refund" [pm-replace].
- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a swap is the club's, at least 2 h before, and a removed paid player is refunded [j-swap]; yet the Terms charge a player who "is replaced" [j-terms-noshow].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** Atras, Miazga and Squash4You waive a late cancellation only if someone else books the court [l-cutoff]; Stacja Nowa Gdynia likewise under 12 h [SIT-51].
- **Provider [DOCS]:** the refund comes out of the club's balance; short of it a card refund waits, a BLIK or P24 refund fails [l-refund-balance]; card 5–10 business days, BLIK within hours [l-refund-speed].
- **Complaints:** "the cancelling player can lose their money while the spot is reopened for somebody else" [j-late-blocks]; "Earn Penalty fee for Late canceling. The Spot is free again" [c-dropped].

## 6. The others, when the seat is taken (R-RESCUE-23)

- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** no notice that someone joined an open match; only a Community Match creator is told "When a player signs up" [j-not-told] [o-not-told].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore: "Ktoś dołączył, gra potwierdzona, gra anulowana - gracze wiedzą wszystko bez udziału recepcji" [p-waitlist].

## 7. A refused rescue payment (R-RESCUE-24 to 28)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** failures are listed (funds, CVC, expired card); "paid… but the match does not appear… the bank… temporarily withholds the amount, wait a few days" [n-pay-fails].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore gives an unpaid booking 30 minutes ("Opłać do {time} · Zostało {minutes} min"), then checks the slot is still free; declines name a reason ("brak środków / limit karty / karta nieważna") [p-lapse].
- **Provider [DOCS]:** a card hold lapses after 4 days 18 h to 7 days; cancelling an uncaptured payment costs nothing; BLIK and P24 cannot be held [l-hold].
- **Complaints:** "Pieniądze na koncie zablokowane, ale płatność nieudana"; a BLIK payment "ciągle oczekująca bez możliwości opłacania ponownie" (Playmore) [c-charged]; a hold that "can stay on your account for up to a week" [o-held].

## 8. Two seats open, one taken by the cut-off (R-RESCUE-29, 30)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a match still short about 1 h before is cancelled and all refunded in full [j-anemone]; club swaps must land before that line [j-swap].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore warns a leaver the game is off for all if nobody takes the seat by the cut-off [p-leave], and shows a full game as "Skład pełny · potwierdzimy o {time}" until then [p-held].

## 9. Other yeses and the week (R-RESCUE-31, 32)

- **Competitor [COMPLAINT]/[ABSENT]:** a reviewer reports at most 2 games at once without Premium; the help shows no limit [n-how-many].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Padel Mates: "You've reached the maximum number of activities you can join for this club" [pm-replace]; Playmore caps a creator's active games [p-settings]; no platform has a booking waiting for its club [noconfirm-any].

## 10. Fill in alerts (R-RESCUE-35)

- **Competitor [POLICY]/[ABSENT]:** one free priority alert (unlimited with Premium) for "available courts or last-minute cancellations" by place, date and time; whether it fires for a seat in an existing match is not stated [j-priority]; Premium matches are pushed as Golden Matches [j-golden].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore alerts by sport, level and daypart, no price field [p-waitlist]; Padel Mates pushes an open match "that matches your level and location" [pm-what].
- **Complaints:** a freed-court alert "niby jest włączone, a nigdy nie przychodzi" (Playmore) and "O powiadomieniach gdy kort się zwolni też można zapomnieć" (tenis4U); "There should be a notification that goes to the waitlist too" (Padel Mates) [c-waitlist]; notices duplicated or untunable [c-notifs].

## 11. You're In and Withdraw (R-RESCUE-36, 37, 38)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a match not yet full can be left any time before it starts, with a full refund [j-leave-any] [j-refund-full].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore: "Wypisz się — gra zostaje" [p-leave]; MATCHi's waiting list costs nothing to join [m-wait].
- **Complaints:** "it's not easy to find my next match. Would be great to see it right on the main screen" [j-next-game].

## 12. A seat taken after the cut-off (R-RESCUE-40)

- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** no warning before paying that the free window has passed [j-checkout].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore's preview states the free-leave window before the join [p-card].
- **Law [LAW]:** standard terms bind only if delivered before the contract (art. 384 KC) [l-terms-bind].
- **Complaints:** the leaving rule differs from what the app showed [c-unclear].
