# LEAVE — leaving, the cut-off, a game short at it, a game played short

Digest for R-LEAVE-1 to 37, 2026-10-08; no new facts. Each line cites the topic section holding the link and read date. Playtomic has no Kraków club [j-krakow].

## 1. Before leaving: the button and the quoted cost (R-LEAVE-1, 2, 3, 12, 13, 14)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** Playtomic: red cross on your photo, then *cancel booking*; a match not yet full can be left until it starts, a full one until 24 h before, then the app offers no leave [j-leave-any].
- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** no warning at checkout once the free window has passed [j-checkout]; the card names no deadline [j-no-deadline]. A private booking's checkout gives a date: "Before Friday 13 February | 11:30: You will receive a refund… After…: Non-cancelable" [o-cancel-private].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore quotes the consequence on the leave: past the free window "…wróci tylko wtedy, gdy gra się nie odbędzie"; on a full game "Jeśli do {cutoff} nikt nie zajmie Twojego miejsca, gra zostanie odwołana dla całego składu" [p-leave]. Its join preview lists the free-leave window and call-off hour [p-card].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** the rule is the club's regulamin: free to 24 h, later the money is kept [l-cutoff].
- **Complaints:** the rule applied differs from the one shown [c-unclear].

## 2. Leaving before the cut-off: refund, card fee, email (R-LEAVE-4, 5, 6)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a timely leave refunds everything, service fee included, to the original method [j-refund-full]: 43,99 zł on a 40 zł spot, where Raketa returns 38,40 zł [j-fee].
- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a card refund "can take 2-15 business days", a wallet one is instant [j-refund-days]; a cancellation email "cannot be disabled" [j-on-joining].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** MATCHi refunds before its cut-off less the platform fee [m-venue]; Playmore refunds to its wallet only, "Opłata serwisowa nie podlega zwrotowi" [p-wallet]; Padel Mates: full refund to 24 h, "Policies may vary by club" [pm-what].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** tenis4U and Garden Padel refund less a 2 % payment fee [l-2pct]; Squash4You moves a timely cancellation to the next booking [l-cutoff].
- **Provider [DOCS]:** "Stripe's processing fees from the original transaction aren't returned" [l-fees]; card about 5–10 business days, BLIK within hours, P24 up to 3 business days [l-refund-speed].
- **Law [LAW]:** a fixed cancelling sum is presumed abusive (art. 385³ pkt 16 KC); UOKiK lets a trader keep only costs "faktycznie poniesionym" [l-abusive].
- **Complaints:** a fee on a timely leave ("0.50€ según la app… me han cobrado 2,16€!") [c-fee-before].

## 3. Leaving after the cut-off (R-LEAVE-7, 8, 9)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** inside 24 h of a full match only the club can let her go [j-late-club]; it decides, refunding "commonly" for weather, injury or illness [cl-late]. The seat is not freed: swaps are the club's, at least 2 h before [j-swap], and the Terms charge a player who "is replaced" [j-terms-noshow].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore and MATCHi return nothing past their windows unless the game is off [p-leave] [m-venue]. Padel Mates is closest to Raketa: if someone joins through the leaver's link, the leaver is "automatically removed" and refunded [pm-replace].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** late means the full price; Atras, Miazga and Squash4You waive it only if someone else books the court [l-cutoff]; Stacja Nowa Gdynia likewise under 12 h [SIT-51]; the booker owes the whole court [l-booker].
- **Complaints:** "Keep the cancellation fee, but let the player leave the match and reopen the spot for someone else" [c-dropped]; no exception for illness ("Who gets a medical report for a cold?!") [c-illness].
- **Last-minute game (R-LEAVE-9):** Playtomic warns of no passed window [j-checkout]; Playmore states it before the join [p-card].

## 4. Leaving while the club decides (R-LEAVE-10, 11)

- **[ABSENT]:** no booking waits for the club at Playtomic [cl-no-confirm] or any platform read [noconfirm-any].
- **Nearest [POLICY]:** Playtomic's spot request: nobody pays before every player's yes, and "Spots are not reserved during the approval process" [j-request].

## 5. The others learn of it (R-LEAVE-15, 16, 17, 18)

- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** no push or email says someone left an open match; only a Community Match creator is told [j-not-told] [o-not-told]. The match chat is public, so a leaver can still read it [j-chat-public].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore notifies "Ktoś dołączył, gra potwierdzona, gra anulowana" [p-waitlist].
- **Status quo:** the leaver posts in the WhatsApp group, which reaches only that group [c-chasing].
- **Complaints:** "they hasn't let me know about last-minute cancellations" [j-lastmin].

## 6. The cut-off itself (R-LEAVE-19, 20, 33)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** an open match's line is a fixed 24 h the club cannot change [cl-24h]; for courts the club sets the hours, no default [cl-rules]; whether a change reaches existing bookings is not stated, the Terms tie the policy to the act of booking [cl-changed].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore: call-off 1–48 h and free cancel 0–72 h, defaults 2 h and 12 h, reach of a change not stated [p-settings]; MATCHi: "Match Settings only affect new matches — existing matches keep their values" [m-venue].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** 24 h at Garden Padel, Fame Sport, Atras, Squash4You, Miazga, KSOS; 12 h at SAO; 48 h for Fame Sport's weekly slots [l-cutoff]; 5 to 24 h elsewhere [SIT-52]; a club changing its hours after booking [SIT-40].
- **Calendar [LAW]:** clocks go back on Sun 25 Oct 2026: 24 h before a 10:00 game is Sat 11:00 [SIT-22].
- **Display [POLICY]:** Playmore's card counts down "Komplet do {time} · Zostało {h} godz. {m} min"; Playtomic's shows no deadline [p-card].

## 7. A seat still open at the cut-off (R-LEAVE-21 to 25, 28, 32)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a match still short "about 1 hour before", or with no court left, is "Cancelled by Anemone" with "a full automatic refund (including the Playtomic service fee)" [o-auto-cancel]; a seat freed before 24 h just reopens [o-refill]; a club removal must land "before the 1-hour cutoff" [j-swap].
- **Competitor [ABSENT]/[POLICY]:** the cancellation email's words and reason are not documented [j-anemone]; the club's admins get email only for "Bookings created or cancelled by players", if they opt in [cl-emails].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore is all or nothing [p-open], wallet refund less its fee [p-wallet], a full game confirmed only at the cut-off ("Skład pełny · potwierdzimy o {time}") [p-held]; MATCHi's optional auto-cancel refunds in full "with a notification" [m-venue].
- **Fees [DOCS]:** the payment fee is the club's, not returned on a refund [l-fees]; refunds come from the club's balance; short of it a card refund waits, BLIK or P24 fails [l-refund-balance].
- **Complaints:** cancelled "despite having 3 registered players" [j-dont-fill]; or no auto-cancel, and the organiser pays the court [c-org-pays].

## 8. Marks on the record (R-LEAVE-26, 27, 28)

- **Competitor [ABSENT]:** no attendance score, strike count or ban threshold [j-no-score].
- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a player who neither left 24 h ahead nor came is reported within 7 days and owes the court as a debt that blocks the app [j-noshow].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** Miazga blocks a no-show until paid; kluby.org shares a club's block, two lock the account [l-noshow-block] [k-blocks].
- **Complaints:** a club's warning given "bez poinformowania użytkownika" blocks booking at every club (tenis4U) [c-unfair].

## 9. A game that goes ahead short (R-LEAVE-29, 30, 31)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a late leaver's seat stays paid, so the club keeps the court [j-late-club]; with a late player the group may "Wait and Play" ("considered fully played. No refunds") [j-terms-noshow]; a result counts only "if all four players… are included" [j-results].
- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a short match the club forces a court onto "won't be automatically cancelled", payer not stated [o-auto-cancel].
- **Status quo [POLICY]:** the booker owes the whole court [l-booker].

## 10. The club cancels after a leave; nobody calls a game off (R-LEAVE-36, 37)

- **Competitor [POLICY]:** a club cancellation refunds in full, fee included, or to the Club Wallet; what an earlier leaver gets is not stated [cl-club-cancel].
- **Competitor [POLICY]:** no player can cancel a whole open match, only the club [o-powers]; a private booking's owner can cancel it whole but cannot leave it [o-owner-leaving].
- **Platforms [POLICY]:** Playmore passes the organiser's role on; to end a game "Po prostu się wypisz - gra bez kompletu odwoła się automatycznie" [p-leave].

## 11. A paused yes coming back (R-LEAVE-34, 35)

- **Platforms [POLICY]:** no waiting list enrols or charges: Playmore tells all at once, "kto pierwszy, ten gra" [p-waitlist]; MATCHi notifies all and enrols nobody [m-wait].
- **Complaints:** "why not pay to join and payment is there in case someone leaves - you get auto added to event" [c-waitlist].
- **Law [DOCS]/[LAW]:** a charge without the player's act needs a recorded agreement on transactions, timing and amount [l-mit]; the binding button must say payment is owed, even if it comes later (C-400/22) [l-button].
