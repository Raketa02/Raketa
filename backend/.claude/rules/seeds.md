# Seed data

Moved verbatim from `backend/CLAUDE.md` (2026-09-30); decisions that are load-bearing and not obvious from the code.

- **Seeds execute use cases, never SQL.** `app/seeds/` creates deterministic development
  accounts through the same use cases a request runs, wired from the composition root, so
  a seeded aggregate has the event stream and the outbox rows a real one has. A structural
  walk refuses a statement import or a `session.add` in that package. The production
  refusal is checked **on import** of `app/seeds/`, not in `make seed` — a make target is
  not what runs inside a container. See the README's *Seed Data*.
- **A seed account is named for a state, and the name is checked.** The catalog is a list
  of edges — nothing yet, one open run, one abandoned, a rating at the edge of its band —
  and a rated scenario carries the band it promises, checked against the stored rating on
  every run. Four of the eleven are **protagonists** carrying a per-player *profile* axis,
  and a Play screen is developed signed in as one of them.
- **Behind the named accounts is the cohort, and it is a workbench that says so on its
  face** (`app/seeds/cohort.py`). A population is generated from a seed and committed as
  `app/seeds/cohort.json` — **inputs only**, never a level, an uncertainty or a band, and
  every draw keyed off the member's own handle so adding one changes that row and nobody
  else's (`make gen-cohort`, and a test asserts byte-identity). It borrowed the ruleset
  corpus's mechanics (gone with the ranking, #579/#580) and **none of its numbers**: that
  corpus was a comparator and this one's job is to *reach* states, which is why ADR-0121 is
  undisturbed. It is the crowd the Games deck, Fill in and posting deal from. It fails
  `make seed` on **two clocks**: an engine retune moves the band histogram (a range per
  band, never a μ — a μ would pin the engine), and the calendar walks every newcomer out of
  ADR-0113's window, which fails **by name with the age in days**. The run injects a
  `Clock` — a port, so ADR-0029 is honoured rather than bent. **Its tags are drawn from all
  twenty-seven, a close match filling up to the served eight** (#832): adding ADR-0207 §7's
  eight redrew every member's tags — tags alone moved in `cohort.json`, reviewed as a fixture
  diff — and Same vibe's six were picked again to meet the new draw. **A store seeded before a
  redraw keeps its old draw** (the commit is idempotent), so the runner gives every member
  whose stored tags aren't their row's the row's through *Edit tags*' own `ChangeSocialTags`
  at the run's instant (`_ensure_the_drawn_tags`; recording nothing once they are), and the
  report counts them. Seed only what is
  **modeled**, and the fence is expressed as **writes against reads** rather than as
  imports (`tests/unit/test_seed_runner.py`).
- **The photographs are a declared verdict and generated bytes** (`app/seeds/photos.py`):
  `PhotoReview` is a port the fixture supplies like the clock, because what a check
  concluded about a synthetic image is a thing a workbench chooses rather than discovers —
  the note itself is written by the domain's own constructors, so a seeded shield is one
  `decision_on_record` agrees to believe. The object store is the one thing the seed writes
  that lives outside the database, so the run **asks it one question first** and reports
  rather than dies when it cannot answer.
- **At least one live club has indoor and outdoor courts of one sport at the same Rakieta
  hours** (#671, Q122), and the run refuses a seed with none
  (`_refuse_a_seed_with_no_club_of_both_kinds`): Bonarka Arena's padel Court 4 is outdoor
  beside Courts 1–3 indoors — her game books Court 1 and may move to 2 or 3, never 4, and a
  padel card there promises neither kind — and Ogrody 33's tennis books Kort centralny,
  outdoors, first, which may move indoors to Court 4. The report names each such club's
  sports.
- **What the seed promises about the screens is proved by reading them**
  (`app/seeds/screens.py`): the group deck, Fill in, the posting grid and the club's
  Bookings day are each read through their own use case as the protagonist or her club,
  after the outbox is drained, and an empty one fails the run by name. The matcher's
  battery — the pool search, the deck, the radar and the eleven relationships — went with
  the challenges (#578). None of this runs in CI: it needs a database with the seed in it.
- **Every played game is a past booked game, on the path the product makes games on**
  (`app/seeds/booked_history.py`, #573). That covers her own evenings (tennis singles that
  fill the record she reads about herself) and the cohort's `PLAYED_GAMES`, which the
  runner hands in. Each game is made, filled by its last yes, played and scored through
  the use cases a phone runs, on a backdated clock, in a group she runs per sport at
  Ogrody 33. Five things are load-bearing.
  - **Everybody on a counted game played it competitively** (Q127, #738): a yes is held to
    the kind its player's intent wants, read live, so somebody on one whose own intent in
    the sport is For fun — hers in tennis, and three of the cohort's — **played it
    competitively and switched after**. Their intent is declared competitive before the
    games are made and their own again once every game is written, both at the run's
    instant and from `PROFILE_SCREEN`, the You tab (`HISTORY_INTENT`, `PLAYED_FOR_FUN`); a
    declaration that changes nothing records nothing, so a second run records neither, and
    every screen reads the fixture's intent.
  - **Every game is over before this week began.** A booked game counts toward its week's
    *games per week*, so a game this week would hold the deck, Fill in and posts to a
    smaller week. A declared game keeps its day where that is before the week, and starts
    from the last day before it otherwise. The one exception is a game somebody could not
    have played before the week, because they committed inside it. That game is played on
    its declared day, and the report counts them. Every game also ends at least two days
    before the run, so every result window on it has closed.
  - **The fill draws the sides**, so a declared doubles row says who was on the court and
    who won, never who partnered whom. **It holds the shares, and the cut-off job is run at
    each game's cut-off** on its backdated clock (#637), so every past game is taken and
    settled as the API's job would have left it.
  - **Levels move through the rating catch-up**, the API's job. Cards are answered the day
    after their evening (`sportsmanship.RATED_AFTER`), inside their week on any weekday.
  - **`assert_her_history` fails the run by name** if any of these is missing:
    - a counted evening, read on its details — every evening is past what Home sends
      (#613);
    - a confirmed counted result of hers for the catch-up to fold;
    - a rater who rated her on one of her evenings.
- **On the Stripe sandbox the seed moves its money through Stripe** (#712,
  `app/seeds/money.py`). On the fake nothing changed: each layer asks for a fake of its
  own, as before. With `PAYMENTS_PROVIDER=stripe` and a test key, every layer that moves
  money — the cards, the clubs' accounts, every seeded game's shares and the outbox drain
  — gets the run's one Stripe adapter from the composition root, and no module but
  `money.py` names a provider builder (`test_no_layer_builds_a_provider_of_its_own`). Two
  stand-ins play what a person does on Stripe's own pages:
  - **the phone** confirms each setup server-side with Stripe's test PaymentMethod for
    what the declared card does (`SANDBOX_METHODS`: the demand suite's
    `STRIPE_TEST_METHODS` keyed by behaviour, held equal by a test) — so a card pays, or
    declines later, as declared, and reads Visa •••• 4242 whatever brand, last four and
    wallet the mock draws;
  - **the onboarding pages**: a ready club is connected to the account a person
    onboarded for it (`OnboardedAccounts`), named in `SEED_STRIPE_CLUB_ACCOUNTS` —
    `Club name=acct_…` pairs separated by commas, read from the process environment like
    `SEED_CENTRE`, never from `Settings` — and reads ready because Stripe says so. Wisła
    Park opens a new account and stays *not started*.

  **How**: onboard one sandbox account per ready club — Ogrody 33, Centrum Ruchu,
  Kazimierz Padel, Bonarka Arena, Zabłocie Sport, Prądnik Tennis, Nowa Huta Squash — in
  Stripe's hosted pages; put `PAYMENTS_PROVIDER=stripe`, the test keys and the mapping in
  `.env.docker`; then `make down-v && make up && make seed` (`make seed-host` takes the
  mapping from the shell). **One account per club, not shared**: the store holds one club
  per provider account (`club_payments_accounts`' primary key), so a second club on one
  account would fail half way, and the run refuses it first. **Refused before anything is
  written**, by name: a key that is not a test key; the mapping unset, malformed, naming
  a club not declared ready, missing a ready one, or giving two clubs one account; an
  account whose charges or payouts the sandbox has not enabled. **A sandbox hold lasts
  7 days** (measured on its Visa; a live Visa's lasts 4 days 18 hours), so the crowd's far-ahead game looks more than
  eight days ahead there (`OnTheSandbox.far_ahead`) — five, the fake's, would hold its
  seats instead of checking them. **Two runs within the hour stay correct**: a key is the
  act and the hour, and every id it carries — a player, a club, a game, a charge — is
  minted by the store, never chosen by the seed, so a fresh store asks Stripe no key an
  earlier one asked (`tests/api/test_seed_money.py` runs the whole seed twice at one
  instant through one fake and finds none in common). Out of reach there: the console's
  provider controls (`provider_not_the_fake`) and anything measured in days. Stripe is
  asked by `tests/api/test_seed_on_the_sandbox.py` only where the test keys and the
  mapping are both placed, and skipped by name otherwise.
